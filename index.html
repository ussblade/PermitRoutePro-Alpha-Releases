Make the permit route visible before the trip begins.

Permit Route Pro is an Android app for oversize and overweight permit routing. Import a permit, review and correct the mapped route, save the accepted route record, and resume the verified trip for navigation.

From permit scanning to verified-route navigation.

Permit in

Add the original PDF or permit images. The source stays attached to the trip.

Route found

Review extracted roads, turns, origin, destination, and restrictions in plain language.

Path accepted

Compare every waypoint with the permit, make corrections, then store the audit record.

Trip resumed

Open the verified trip and navigate along the permit-approved path.

Partial support varies by state and permit format; compare every route with the issued permit before travel. In-progress states have routing work under development. Routing is not yet available in the remaining states.
